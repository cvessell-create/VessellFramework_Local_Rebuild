# VessellFramework Scientific Evidence Skill

## Status, need and authority

Version 0.1, October 7, 2026. RESEARCH METHOD / CALIBRATION PENDING.
Prepared using the [Skill Creator](VesselFramework_Skill_Creator_SKILL_v0.1.md).
The recurring need is to prevent mathematical vocabulary, software tests,
human inquiry completion and blockchain receipts from promoting unsupported
scientific claims. This skill supplies an evidence method, not a sixth pillar.
Harm Gate, Evidence Assurance, Provenance Firewall and author-source preservation
retain precedence. Creation/loading is not empirical promotion.

Use for scientific support claims, model selection, framework/skill revisions,
case-study evaluation, forecasts and provenance/attestation claims.
Do not force a mathematical model into a task that does not need one.
Do not describe a social field as a physical field without an independently
supported measurement model.

## Input contract

- Bounded decision and exact claim, pillar, domain, population and time.
- Source/version/locator and access level: reviewed section, abstract only,
  metadata only, failed access or secondary lead.
- Data origin, units, sampling/selection and measurement error.
- Proposed model and assumptions; alternative explanations and missing inputs.
- Consent, sensitivity, authority and consequences of error.
- Desired evidence type and test/verification method.

## Procedure

1. Identify the smallest claim that needs support. Tag it SOURCE-ESTABLISHED,
   FRAMEWORK SYNTHESIS, WORKING HYPOTHESIS or ILLUSTRATIVE.
2. Open the primary source before adopting its findings; verify bibliographic
   details. Failed access is not reading. Metadata establishes a publication
   record, not its findings. A paper's references are not independent checks.
3. Map each model to its original scope. Record assumptions, units,
   identifiability, measurement validity and failure conditions. An analogy
   may motivate a test but cannot establish the case conclusion.
4. Separate observed association, causal identification and intent attribution.
   A causal claim requires a defensible design/model; intent requires its own
   evidence. Neither congestion nor discrepancy alone identifies unwillingness
   or deception.
5. State the evidence actually obtained and the claim it can support:
   - **Digest:** byte identity/integrity relative to a trusted reference.
   - **External timestamp:** verified prior-existence bound for a commitment.
   - **Signature:** key authorization under an independently established binding.
   - **Reproducible computation:** repeatable procedure/output under declared
     inputs/environment; not truth of the inputs.
   - **Empirical result:** measured effect in the tested design/population with
     uncertainty, limitations and adverse/null findings.
   - **Conditional mathematical result:** conclusion under explicit assumptions
     with a checkable derivation/proof; not proof those assumptions hold.
   These are different evidence dimensions, not interchangeable grades.
6. Specify disconfirmers, baseline, resolution rule, sample/power rationale,
   uncertainty method and stop conditions before evaluation. Use held-out
   cases; do not select only the cases that inspired the skill.
7. Preserve separate human perspective accounts and unavailable/declined
   participation. Record completion is not embodied presencing or validation.
8. Produce the bounded dossier below. Preserve failures, dissent, corrections
   and supersession; do not rewrite old hashes or historical source documents.
9. Apply Harm Gate and human release policy separately. No proof, payment,
   consensus, score or digital credit grants execution authority.
10. For prompt-steering studies, use consented, user-exported visible records
    and clearly labeled editor metadata. Never infer private chain-of-thought
    from rationales, edits, timestamps, or outcome correlations. Record the
    capture boundary, selection process, missing turns, and data-retention
    choice; do not keylog or scrape private editor/chat stores.

## Reciprocal strategic-model check

When a case involves interdependent actor choices, a game-theoretic model may
be considered after source, context, feasibility, observation and perspective
checks. Require evidence-linked players/actions/information, declared utility
units, assumptions, horizon and missing actors. Model outputs are conditional
mathematics, not empirical observations or motive evidence. Feed the result
back through the five other pillars; revise, abstain or report NOT_SUPPLIED if
the model boundary or assumptions do not survive. Do not tune utilities to
produce a preferred outcome, and do not mistake scenario sensitivity for
calibrated probability.

External sources on linked cooperative games in ecosystems or
evolutionary/social-ecological change supply possible analytical concepts only.
Record their access level and limits in the
[scientific foundations crosswalk](docs/pillar-scientific-foundations.md);
neither source establishes the validity, fairness or efficacy of this
framework's adaptation.

## Six-pillar checks

| Pillar | Model/test handoff | Prohibited leap |
|---|---|---|
| PARADOX | Context-indexed propositions; source/measurement alternatives. | Conflicting reports prove deception. |
| BOTTLENECK | Queue/capacity model with compatible units and stable boundary; causal design when claiming an effect. | A queue proves WILL NOT, or Little's Law alone locates a constraint. |
| DUAL LAYER | Explicit observation/latent-state model and identifiability/sensitivity checks. | Missing observations prove a hidden reality or source condition. |
| XFACTOR | Predeclared scenarios, bounded losses, frozen probabilities and proper scoring. | Surprise, narrative plausibility or presencing establishes calibrated probabilities. |
| KNOWING FIELD | Human-led reflexive inquiry; consent/dissent and independently specified outcome study. | Schema completeness, entropy or group agreement measures consciousness. |
| GAME THEORY | Evidence-linked actors, feasible actions, information, utility units, alternatives and sensitivity; conditional results return to the other five checks. | A computed equilibrium reveals real preferences, predicts unobserved conduct, establishes fairness or authorizes action. |

## Digital evidence dossier output

Record, using the existing evidence/provenance identifiers where possible:

```text
claim_id / pillar / exact_claim / claim_status
source_id / version / locator / access_level / contribution / source_limits
model / variables_units / assumptions / identifiability / alternatives
test_plan / baseline / disconfirmers / resolution_rule / uncertainty
result / null_adverse_results / validation_scope / unresolved_gaps
artifact_path / encoding / digest_algorithm / digest
signature_status / identity_binding / anchor_status / independent_verification
human_inquiry_revision / release_status / supersedes / next_test
```

This is an authoring template, not a new runtime schema or automatic output.
Use NOT_PERFORMED/NOT_AVAILABLE for absent tests, signatures and anchors.
A pending anchor is not verified. A local clock is not independent time evidence.
Keep sensitive content private; even hashes can be linkable or guessable.

## Tests and promotion

Before promoting a derived skill, check that it:

- Rejects a hash as proof of truth, authorship, payment or theoretical validity.
- Rejects unreviewed search citations and metadata-only substantive findings.
- Declares mathematical assumptions and preserves unknown/violated assumptions.
- Keeps causal estimates and intent claims separate from associations.
- Preserves null/adverse results, missing perspectives and correction history.
- Preserves human review and authority boundaries even when a task is paid.
- Shows prospective comparative evidence rather than software pass counts alone.

The loader can verify that these instructions are supplied; it cannot prove a
model follows them or that the five-pillar method improves human outcomes.
Promotion remains dependent on the planned empirical evaluation.
