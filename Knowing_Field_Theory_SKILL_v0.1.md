## Purpose and recurring need

Examine what a predominantly outside-observer analysis omits: the observer's
participation, lived and dialogic perspectives, relational source conditions,
freedom to dissent and the movement from attention to intention and agency.
Do not equate a relationship graph with fourth-person knowing.

## Triggers and non-triggers

Use when framing a consequential report, designing a human-agent workflow,
revising doctrine, auditing observer blindness or evaluating collective change.
Do not use to infer an owner's personality, force spiritual agreement,
generate an experience on someone's behalf or decorate an otherwise complete
mechanical answer. Scope can be narrow, but report-release inquiry cannot be
silently omitted under the owner's selected policy.

## Input contract

Decision question; bounded social/system context and observation time;
observer role and intervention; affected parties and participation state;
source-linked human accounts, dialogue and observations; explicit knowledge
gaps; power and consent constraints; alternatives; intended consequences.
Missing subjective/dialogic accounts must remain missing, with limitations.
Never solicit private phenomenological detail as a condition of service.

## Analytical sequence

1. **Suspend:** separate observations, reports, interpretations and assumptions.
   Name the current frame, inherited categories and the observer's contribution.
2. **Redirect:** invite voluntary human reflection and dialogue where appropriate.
   Record absent voices, declined participation and collection constraints.
3. **Distinguish:** preserve first-person reports, second-person dialogue,
   third-person observations and fourth-person interpretations as different
   evidence forms, not a single confidence score.
4. **Attend to source conditions:** inquire into relationships, implicit
   assumptions, incentives, asymmetry and ecological or wider consequences.
   Distinguish manifest events from proposed generative conditions.
5. **Reframe:** identify how the observed whole includes the inquiry itself.
   Preserve contradictory accounts, alternatives and unresolved blind spots.
6. **Articulate intention:** state the human-selected purpose without claiming
   that a field, model or majority supplied legitimate authority.
7. **Bound agency:** identify actor, permission, reversibility, stop conditions
   and a testable next step. Keep emergence and forecasts distinct.
8. **Evaluate:** retain baseline and follow-up evidence; allow adverse or
   null results to challenge the adaptation. Do not erase failures.

## Method mosaic and handoffs

The external contribution is fourth-person theory and human inquiry.
The framework contributes provenance, alternatives, constraints, Harm Gate
and decision authority. The skill contributes a documentary inquiry contract.
These contributions are not independent proofs of one another.

`HUMAN ACCOUNT -> PROVENANCE -> PERSPECTIVE DISTINCTION -> FIELD INTERPRETATION
-> ALTERNATIVES -> HUMAN ASSESSMENT -> EXISTING GATES -> EVALUATION`

Reject automatic sentiment/personality inference, a numerical consciousness
score, collective consensus as truth, and a fifth-person hierarchy. None is
needed to operationalize the requested inquiry.

## Output and decision rules

Output a bounded field map: observer position, affected parties, perspective
accounts/gaps, dissent, blind spots, attention, intention, agency, alternatives,
evidence references, limitations and evaluation plan.
Label interpretations FRAMEWORK SYNTHESIS or WORKING HYPOTHESIS.
Completion means a human documented the inquiry, not that fourth-person
knowing occurred, truth was established or external action was authorized.

## Failure, stop and Harm Gate

Stop or retain review when the scope is unspecified, accounts are fabricated,
participation is coerced, dissent is erased, source links are invalid or
consequences cannot be bounded. Sparse evidence permits honest limitations,
not invented testimony. Review privacy, academic disclosure, coercion,
reputational exposure and reversibility alongside the existing Harm Gate.
