## Purpose and authority

For prompt-trajectory audits, keep the owner's explicit self-report, visible
assistant output, analyst synthesis, and any independently observed result as
separate perspectives with separate provenance. Record revision history and
missing turns; do not translate a prompt sequence into a claim about the
owner's private mental state or a generated rationale into access to model
internals. Apply the protocol in
[`docs/prompt-steering-and-reasoning-audit.md`](docs/prompt-steering-and-reasoning-audit.md).

The current architecture also includes
[GAME THEORY as its sixth pillar](VesselFramework_Game_Theory_SKILL_v0.1.md).
Declared incentives and conditional solver output inform inquiry, never
replace human perspective accounts or satisfy this release gate.

KNOWING FIELD asks: **How do the observer, affected participants, relationships,
absent perspectives and emerging possibilities shape this analysis and its
consequences, and who may legitimately act?**

It complements rather than displaces PARADOX, BOTTLENECK, DUAL LAYER and
XFACTOR. Harm Gate, Evidence Assurance, Provenance Firewall and author-source
preservation retain precedence. The policy requires a human-completed fifth
pillar before any new human report release, including pending legacy jobs.
No retrospective rewriting or automatic assessment of historical releases.

## Sixth-pillar strategic-model bridge

Use the [GAME THEORY skill](VesselFramework_Game_Theory_SKILL_v0.1.md) and
[audit](docs/game-theory-framework-audit.md) for the strategic-model handoff.
Human field inquiry informs model boundaries and participant coverage,
observer position and model choices, declared assumptions, and interpretation
of conditional outputs. Record affected people outside the modeled actors,
missing or declined perspectives, participation consent and dissent; do not
turn unavailable accounts into inferred preferences or agreement. Keep human
accounts distinct from modeled actors, actions, information and utilities.

This bridge is VessellFramework synthesis, not an external validation claim.
Game-theory outputs do not substitute for human inquiry, create consent, infer
motives, raise confidence or validate outcomes. They cannot bypass human
completion bound to the actual source and exact preview, the separate human
release decision, Harm Gate or execution authority.

## Source adoption and modification

Scharmer and Pomeroy (2024), *Fourth person: The knowing of the field*,
https://doi.org/10.47061/jasc.v4i1.7909, pp. 19-48, provide the external lens.
Full coverage and PDF fingerprint are in the theory skill.
Their article is an action-research-informed epistemological proposal and
explicitly distinguishes embodied presencing from generative AI capability.
The five phenomena are not software metrics.

The mandatory human completion gate, structured record, source binding,
revision rules and cross-pillar integration are **VessellFramework synthesis**,
not requirements of the paper or independently validated science.
Human reflection may be absent or declined. Documentation of limitations
counts as honest inquiry, not as a finding that all perspectives were accessed.

## Triggers, non-triggers and input contract

Apply to analytical framing, report preparation/release, doctrine revision,
workflow design and significant human-agent decisions. Mechanical substeps
may reuse an enclosing assessment; never use this skill to bypass other gates.
It is not a personality, consciousness, spirituality or consensus detector.

Machine contract: [field-inquiry schema](schemas/field-inquiry.schema.json).
Required: bounded context; observer position; affected parties and participation
state; distinct accounts of the four perspectives; evidence references;
dissent; blind spots; attention; intention; agency; alternatives;
evaluation plan; limitations.
Explicit unavailable/declined accounts are preferable to invented experiences.
Consent applies to inquiry participation, not to fabricated group agreement.

## Sequence and cross-pillar handoffs

`QUESTION -> FIELD BOUNDARY / OBSERVER -> SIGNAL -> PROVENANCE -> PERSPECTIVES
-> PARADOX -> BOTTLENECK -> DUAL LAYER -> XFACTOR -> KNOWING FIELD SYNTHESIS
-> ALTERNATIVES -> AUTHORITY -> CONSEQUENCE -> CONFIDENCE -> HARM GATE
-> HUMAN FIELD COMPLETION -> HUMAN RELEASE DECISION -> EVALUATION`

| Pillar | Required field-aware handoff |
|---|---|
| PARADOX | Distinguish contradiction from different collection positions, interpretive frames and omitted voices. |
| BOTTLENECK | Examine coordination, exclusion and constitutive constraints before assigning incapacity or unwillingness. |
| DUAL LAYER | Distinguish observable manifestation from proposed source conditions; disclose the analyst's intervention. |
| XFACTOR | Convert emergent possibilities into bounded alternatives and disconfirmers, not forecasts or action grants. |
| KNOWING FIELD | Integrate observer participation, perspective gaps, dissent and attention/intention/agency into a documented, correctable human assessment. |
| GAME THEORY | Use human inquiry to bound strategic models and participant coverage, disclose observer/model choices and assumptions, and interpret conditional outputs without replacing missing accounts, consent or dissent. |

1. Read the theory skill; scope the relevant human/system field.
2. Preserve provenance and mark source reports versus interpretations.
3. Invite voluntary reflection/dialogue when appropriate; preserve refusal.
4. Apply original diagnostics only where they add value; do not force every
   pillar into every small task.
5. Record relational blind spots, alternatives, intended agency and limitations.
6. Assess consequences for operator, other affected people and wider systems.
7. An authorized human completes the bounded inquiry against the actual
   input/preview, with optimistic concurrency and durable audit.
8. Separately decide release under Harm Gate and existing authority.
9. Monitor outcomes; correction invalidates assumptions and requires a new
   version/assessment rather than erasing prior evidence.

## Decision and output contract

Outputs include six-pillar framing, inquiry status, source-linked assessment,
unresolved limitations, human completion actor/time/bindings, and next test.
Machine validation means DOCUMENTED_UNREVIEWED, not HUMAN_COMPLETED.
Human completion means procedural completion, not truth or field experience.
Missing inquiry blocks release but not collection, preview, rejection,
diagnostics or preservation. Historical results remain identifiable as legacy.
No waiver through folder metadata, caller declarations or owner task dispatch.

## Failures, harm and adversarial tests

Block release for missing human completion, stale input/preview binding,
invalid evidence IDs, tampered history, stale assessment revision or Harm Gate
failure. Reject unknown fields, unsupported experience claims as machine
capabilities and injected approval/authority fields.
Preserve dissent even after completion; do not equate agreement with consent.

Test T1-T10 from the Skill Creator and specifically:
agent cannot self-complete; sparse input remains incomplete; declined voices
stay declined; shared roots stay shared; alternate explanation can win;
legacy approval returns to review; completion cannot bypass harm;
filing never completes inquiry; specialist/capture/push reuse one contract;
no second confidence algorithm is created.
