## Need and governing order

Use when an outcome depends on other actors' choices, shared thresholds,
scarce allocation, information asymmetry or sequential decisions.
Do not create a strategic model for mere contradiction, mechanical throughput,
an unsupported attribution or a collection of unexplained scores.
Harm Gate, Evidence Assurance, provenance and human release retain precedence.
GAME THEORY is an optional, reciprocal lens: the five other pillars constrain
the model and then recheck its assumptions and outputs. It does not supersede
their evidence, human-perspective, confidence or authority boundaries.

## Inputs and transformations

Require players; available actions; who observes what and when; terminal
conditions; transitions; utility units; provenance; assumptions; missing
perspectives; alternatives; constraints; evaluation and stop conditions.
Declare a finite horizon before planning. Null utility is not zero.
Ordinal preference, eligibility, cost and cardinal utility are different types.

1. **Normal form:** list joint actions and a payoff for each actor. Pure Nash
   means no profitable unilateral deviation in that supplied game, not morally
   correct behavior. Weak dominance permits equality but requires at least one
   strict improvement; strict dominance improves every listed response.
2. **Sequential form:** backward induction maximizes the acting player's
   declared utility at each node. Perfect information is not simultaneous
   choice. An abstraction's policy is not an optimal continuous-state policy.
3. **Coalitional form:** with fixed roster, weights `w`, quota `q` and veto set,
   enumerate winning coalitions. Count each marginal swing and factorial
   pivotal ordering. Keep absolute and normalized Banzhaf distinct.
4. **Reserve:** `R_i=C(B_i+phi_i)/2`. Fixed inputs imply fixed reserve;
   normalized reserve sums to C. Survival, flooring, critical bonuses and caps
   determine a separate realized payout. Two critical awards of 175 may total
   350 although their base reserves total 300.
5. **Savings:** retain the owner's quarter-deposit and half-reserve target.
   `H=min(request,Y+D,max(0,K+D-L))`; `K'=K+D-H`,
   `U'=U+A-D+H`, `Y'=Y+D-H`, `E'=E+A`.
   From zero, `K+U=E`, `Y=K`, and total harvest cannot exceed deposits.
   No spontaneous growth, ecological maximum sustainable yield or XP minting.
6. **Hybrid state:** `S_next=F(S,action,dt,time,random)`; observation `h(S)`
   is a projection. Complete replay also needs ordered accepted actions, clock
   and RNG state. Sampled snapshots do not reconstruct hidden bids or actions.

## Reciprocal six-pillar application

| Pillar | Before a model | Return check on conditional results |
|---|---|---|
| PARADOX | Align claims to context, actors, rules, time and information; distinguish a contradiction from a persistent trade-off. | Confirm that comparisons keep the same boundary; report whether alternate assumptions explain the tension. |
| BOTTLENECK | Establish evidence for feasibility, resource and coordination limits. | Do not infer a binding operational constraint from a modeled incentive; separate feasibility from willingness. |
| DUAL LAYER | Mark preferences/utilities as observed, elicited or analyst-supplied and preserve provenance. | Do not re-label a payoff or equilibrium as behavior, hidden state or motive. |
| XFACTOR | Identify omitted actors, information gaps, changing rules and uncertain outcomes. | Vary assumptions and report sensitivity without inventing calibrated scenario probabilities. |
| KNOWING FIELD | Record observer participation, affected parties, consent, absent/declined perspectives and dissent. | Human inquiry remains separate; machine output cannot supply consent, dialogue or source/preview-bound completion. |
| GAME THEORY | Model only evidence-supported interdependence with declared inputs and scope. | Return assumptions/results for all five checks; revise or abstain if they fail. |

An example full-cycle trace is: **source/evidence → context and alternatives →
feasibility/observation/uncertainty → human perspective review → optional
declared game → bounded calculation → five-pillar challenge → provenance and
uncertainty update → Harm Gate and separate human release decision**. This is
iterative; it is not a mandatory linear order or an instruction to force every
case into a game.

## Cross-domain limits and failure criteria

Weights derived from maximum HP and level are designer inputs, not observed
intelligence, contribution or skill. Reverse tracing follows logged dependencies;
rounding, caps and equal shares destroy uniqueness. Do not infer motives,
party affiliation, deception or a planted narrative from game outputs.

Euler routes, MST, SAT, critical paths, matching, EDA, agenda sensitivity,
May and Gibbard-Satterthwaite are recorded research candidates, not silently
implemented models. An algorithm's Boolean, graph, rank or assignment needs an
explicit unit-bearing adapter before it can become a utility or reward weight.
Unresolved "counter point" and chair references remain unresolved.

Reject missing utilities, unknown sources, malformed or oversized models,
unattainable quotas, unknown actors and unbounded trees. Report non-applicability
or NOT_SUPPLIED rather than fabricate incentives. Do not prune weakly dominated
actions and claim all equilibria are preserved.

## Validation and promotion

Use analytic fixtures, adversarial invalid inputs, independent permutation
checks, conservation properties and perturbations of quotas/payoffs.
Compare alternatives before recommending a policy. For human outcomes,
predefine a baseline, held-out cases, harm criteria, consent and independent
review. Record null/adverse results. Owner adoption is not empirical promotion.
