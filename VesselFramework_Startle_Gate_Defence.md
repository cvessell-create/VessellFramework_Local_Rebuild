# Startle Gate Defence

## Purpose

The Startle Gate is an anti-panic and anti-authority-inflation control for
claims framed as sudden shock, fear, legal trouble, reputational exposure,
ambush, revelation, or an inauspicious forecast.

It does not validate or reject as a cultural or religious
practice. It keeps a source's interpretation in the correct evidence register:
the source may establish that it uses Startle Gate to mean shock,
lawsuits, fear, or unexpected events; that does not establish that a shock,
lawsuit, threat, or outcome exists in the operator's situation.





## Operational rule

`FREEZE -> NAME THE SIGNAL -> PROVENANCE -> TRIANGULATE -> ALTERNATIVES -> HARM GATE -> FORWARD POSTURE -> DECIDE`

The agent must:

1. Freeze irreversible or reputationally costly action.
2. Name the signal without repeating it as fact.
3. Separate `the source states X` from `X is happening`.
4. Locate a time-stamped ordinary-world artifact or direct observation.
5. Seek independent corroboration; derivative copies do not count. Use the Search Gate (`VesselFramework_Search_Gate_SKILL_v0.1.md`) for scoped searches and primary confirmation.
6. Test ordinary explanations, false positives, metaphor, marketing, and
   confirmation bias.
7. Require professional review for legal, health, financial, safety, or
   reputational decisions.
8. Default to `VERIFY` until the evidence threshold is met.

## Agent output contract

Every Startle Gate review should report:

- Startle Gate status: `CLEAR`, `ACTIVE - VERIFY BEFORE ACTION`, `HIGH - HUMAN
  REVIEW REQUIRED`, or `BLOCKED - UNVERIFIED SHOCK CLAIM`;
- source claim and source status;
- ordinary-world facts and their provenance;
- independent corroboration or its absence;
- alternative explanations;
- confidence ceiling and disconfirming evidence;
- safe posture, authorization, rollback path, and stop condition.

An alert increases verification discipline. It never increases evidence quality,
probability, intent, legal authority, or permission to act.
