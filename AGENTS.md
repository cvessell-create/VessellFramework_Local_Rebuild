# Agent instructions

Consult [VISION_AND_SCOPE.md](VISION_AND_SCOPE.md) before architectural changes.
Apply [KNOWING FIELD](VesselFramework_Knowing_Field_SKILL_v0.1.md) as the fifth
pillar across analysis and skill revisions. Require human source/preview-bound
completion before every new report release, including pending legacy jobs.
Agent-generated context and record completeness cannot substitute for that
completion or demonstrate embodied knowing.
Develop VessellFramework as a **full-stack SI sub-agent** whose implemented
core provides callable, provenance-aware evidence analysis to coordinating
agents. The email-inspired workspace is the **human-facing control plane**,
not the primary product or a replacement for the agent service contract.

Maintain distinct boundaries among authenticated task intake, bounded
analysis, durable evidence and provenance history, human review and report
release, and explicitly authorized execution. Persistent filing metadata
must retain independent revisions and audit history; folder membership,
read/unread markers, flags and category colors do not establish evidentiary
truth, alter analytical confidence or confer approval or execution authority.

Use the [specialist contract](docs/specialist-agent.md) and
[GitHub control-panel contract](docs/github-control-panel.md) as the relevant
implementation references. Distinguish the SI product vision from demonstrated
capabilities and preserve the author's original paper and methodological scope.

All coding agents working in this repository must follow
[`.github/copilot-instructions.md`](.github/copilot-instructions.md), including the
Search Gate and its search-record requirements before claims or third-party imports.
