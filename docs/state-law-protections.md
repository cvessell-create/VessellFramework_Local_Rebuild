## California and corrected Washington citation

California's opened Civil Code sections 1633.3, 1633.5 and 1633.7 address
scope/exclusions, agreement and electronic-form recognition. Section 3426.1
defines trade secrets and excludes reverse engineering or independent
derivation alone from improper means.

The previous Washington reference **RCW 19.74** returned citation not found.
Opened RCW **1.80.040**, **1.80.060** and **1.80.900** support the correction
to chapter **1.80**, the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act. The historical
[electronic-transactions document](washington-rcw-19.74.md) keeps its filename,
not its erroneous citation. See also the [trade-secret notice](trade-secrets-notice.md).

## Federal context (2024 U.S. Code edition opened)

- [15 U.S.C. § 7001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap96-subchapI-sec7001.htm):
  electronic-form recognition in interstate/foreign commerce, with consumer
  consent and other limitations; it is not a blanket enforceability guarantee.
- [15 U.S.C. § 7002](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap96-subchapI-sec7002.htm):
  state-law/preemption conditions require separate analysis.
- [18 U.S.C. § 1836](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title18/html/USCODE-2024-title18-partI-chap90-sec1836.htm) and
  [§ 1839](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title18/html/USCODE-2024-title18-partI-chap90-sec1839.htm):
  federal civil-action conditions and definitions, including reasonable secrecy
  measures, economic value and lawful-acquisition distinctions.
