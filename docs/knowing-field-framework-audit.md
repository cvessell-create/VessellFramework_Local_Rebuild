## Need, methods and promotion decision

Recurring problem: reports can preserve source lineage while omitting the
analyst's participation, absent perspectives, consent, relational conditions
and consequences for others. Existing PARADOX/BOTTLENECK/DUAL LAYER/XFACTOR
contribute diagnostics but do not consistently require these records.
The theory skill adds a human inquiry lens; the combined skill adds a shared
documentation and review contract. Neither measures presencing.

The owner selected a mandatory completed inquiry before every new report
release, including existing workflows. This is an authorized policy change,
not a conclusion derived from the paper or an efficacy finding.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Vocabulary-only adoption:** inadequate; leaves release and output gaps.
2. **Software simulates field experience:** rejected; contradicts the article's
   account of AI limitations and cannot supply human phenomenology.
3. **Human inquiry plus machine-verifiable record:** selected; preserves human
   participation and evidence boundaries while making omissions visible.
4. **Force all perspectives to be available:** rejected; induces fabrication
   and may coerce personal disclosure. Explicit absences and declined
   participation are acceptable inquiry findings.
5. **Use collective agreement to raise confidence:** rejected; independence
   and confidence remain governed by evidence assurance.

## Migration and release boundaries

New and existing pending jobs require a human assessment bound to their
immutable event and exact preview. Approval and worker release both verify it.
An already-approved legacy job without an assessment returns explicitly to
human review instead of releasing or pretending success.
Historical COMPLETED reports remain historical; absence of an inquiry record
is displayed as legacy/unassessed, not silently repaired.

CLI case outputs and analytical adapters remain previews with an explicit
field-review requirement. Synthetic workflow/capture/correction completion
does not imply report release. Existing permission gates for scanners,
remediation and owner workflows remain intact; the new inquiry cannot grant
additional authority or rewrite their measured outputs.

## Skill Creator record and validation criteria

Selected mosaic: human fourth-person inquiry; original four diagnostics;
provenance; alternatives; Harm Gate; durable reviewer authority.
Rejected duplication: another confidence engine, personality inference or
additional execution channel.
State: owner-authorized policy / research adaptation / calibration pending.

Acceptance tests cover T1 normal, T2 sparse, T3 dissent, T4 derivative roots,
T5 alternative, T6 legacy/configured state, T7 Harm Gate, T8 irrelevant theory,
T9 multiple case types and T10 shared helper reuse. Tests must distinguish
record validation from empirical or phenomenological validation.
Compare later consented pilots to the four-pillar baseline, including delay,
coercion burden, missed voices, actionable blind spots and null/adverse outcomes.
Do not claim that a deterministic completeness check measures knowing.
