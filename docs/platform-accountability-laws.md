# Platform transparency and accountability: bounded research index

Research snapshot: October 10, 2026. **Federal and all 50 states** were targeted.
This is legal information, **not legal advice** and **not complete protection**.
The broader consumer/privacy/IP mapping remains incomplete. No platform conduct,
infringement, interference attribution or company-specific applicability is established.

## What this can and cannot do

New York's [GBS 1102](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/1102)
describes semiannual reporting of moderation practices and aggregate actions,
including automated systems and human review. Such information can help research
platform representations; it cannot itself prove what happened to a specific
repository, establish motive, or show unlawful use of code.
[GBS 1103](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/1103) reserves enforcement
under that article to the attorney general. [GBS 1100](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/1100)
excludes content uploaded solely for cloud storage, file transmission or collaboration.
Platform classification, revenue thresholds, current court restrictions and application
to a particular case require independent review.

The [Apache license](../LICENSE) remains unchanged. Its copyright grant and
redistribution conditions are separate from platform moderation transparency.
No notice here revokes existing permissions, creates a cause of action or proves
that AI training, copying or another specific use violates the license.

## Evidence boundaries

**SOURCE-ESTABLISHED** means only inspected substantive government text, not
current enforceability. **UNVERIFIED** entries are research leads, including
bills, secondary summaries and unopened sources. Every entry's present
enforceability remains **UNVERIFIED**. Source-edition snapshots are not current-law
certification. A bill landing-page summary is not an enacted statute.

**NOT FOUND IN SCOPE** means no qualifying direct analogue was located in the
bounded search, not that the state has no relevant law. Most state searches were
one targeted discovery query; general consumer, privacy and common-law coverage
is not exhaustive. Inaccessible sources and false initial source labels were
not upgraded to confirmed protection.

Individual SEARCH RECORDs and additional unverified leads are in the
[source register](platform-accountability-laws.json). The recorded discovery
counts are researcher-reported, not independently validated. Queries, tools,
scope, counts, limits, primary access and unchecked subjects remain visible.
Federal references use the **2023 U.S. Code edition**. Enforcement mechanisms,
current bills and judicial status require further primary review.

See also [electronic-transactions and trade-secret references](state-law-protections.md).
No complaint, takedown, legal filing, platform access or license amendment is authorized here.
