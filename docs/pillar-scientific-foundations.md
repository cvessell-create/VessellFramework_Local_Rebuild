## What this research establishes

The five pillars can use established tools from logic, queueing, causal
inference, measurement, information theory, probabilistic forecasting and
human-computer interaction. Those tools have their own assumptions and scope.
Their existence does **not** validate the framework's integrations, original
Maskirovka hypotheses, field efficacy, owner characteristics or AI presencing.

This is a targeted multidisciplinary internet/source review, not an exhaustive
systematic review of all sciences. Broad search results were treated as leads;
direct author, journal, standards and publisher-deposited records were checked.
Some search requests timed out, and some publisher pages/PDF paths were
inaccessible. Access limits are recorded rather than filled with inferred
findings. No new participant study or economic experiment was performed.

## PARADOX: context before contradiction

**Question:** Do two claims genuinely conflict, or do their definitions,
observation times, boundaries or collection positions differ?

Classical logical model: a proposition `P(c)` and its negation `not P(c)` cannot
both be true under the same context `c`. Here `c` includes proposition meaning,
time, population, scope and relevant conditions. This logical constraint is
not a finding that either reporter is lying. Evidence that A reported P and B
reported not-P is evidence of conflicting reports, not proof both underlying
facts hold. Preserve source dependence and measurement error.

**Basis:** S7's logical distinction; S2's restraint about causal explanation.
Organizational paradox may mean persistent competing demands, not classical
logical inconsistency. The metadata-only organizational literature has not
been promoted to a case finding.

**Framework hypothesis:** Context-indexed comparison may reduce false
contradiction and deception attributions. That improvement is untested.

**Test:** Predefine context-matched and context-mismatched case pairs;
independent domain reviewers resolve them against source records. Compare
unsupported-attribution and missed-conflict rates with a provenance-only
baseline. Blind reviewers to method labels where feasible; retain disagreement
and uncertainty. Disconfirmation includes increased false accusations or no
measurable improvement.

## BOTTLENECK: conserved work and causal restraint

**Question:** What measured resource/coordination condition limits useful
throughput within this boundary?

Use `L = lambda * W`: L is mean tasks in the defined system, lambda mean task
arrival rate, W mean time in that system. Units must match. Little's original
abstract states finite means and stationary/ergodic arrival conditions; do not
silently apply this relationship to an unstable backlog or mismatched task
populations. Abandoned/rejected tasks and boundary crossings must be handled
consistently. Inspect variation and censoring, not just means.

Illustrative only: `lambda = 2 tasks/hour` and `W = 3 hours` imply
`L = 6 tasks` if assumptions hold. These are not repository measurements.
The equation is a relationship, not a bottleneck-location algorithm.
For a simple serial, no-bypass process with all capacities expressed in the
same work units, sustained throughput cannot exceed the smallest stage
capacity; parallelism, batching, rework and changing task mix need a different
model. This is an explicit framework model, not a quoted finding from S4.

**Basis:** S4 abstract-level queue theory; S2 causal/association distinction.
Neither long waits nor spare nominal resources identify WILL NOT or intent.

**Test:** Collect arrivals, departures, rejection/abandonment, queue size,
service/review time, utilization and task mix over a specified interval.
Compare targeted capacity/coordination changes using a defensible prospective
design. Record confounders, outages and censoring. Disconfirmation includes
unchanged throughput or displaced harm/bottlenecks. The human-release stage
is a real service-capacity constraint, not permission to remove the inquiry gate.

## DUAL LAYER: observation is not identification

**Question:** Which operational state is supported by observations, and which
remains an unidentifiable explanation of surface signals?

Specify an observation model `Y = h(Z, U) + error`, where Y is an observed
signal, Z a proposed latent state, U declared contextual variables, and h a
specified measurement mapping. This is a model template, not evidence that Z
exists or can be recovered.

A concrete binary measurement example:

```text
r = sensitivity * prevalence + (1 - specificity) * (1 - prevalence)
prevalence = (r + specificity - 1) / (sensitivity + specificity - 1)
```

The inversion needs independently known/stable sensitivity and specificity,
compatible population sampling, `sensitivity + specificity != 1`, and a result
within [0, 1]. Observed positive rate r alone does not identify all three
unknowns. Unknown error rates or selection bias leave the latent prevalence
unresolved. This elementary probability derivation is FRAMEWORK SYNTHESIS,
not an imported theorem validating hidden intent or human source conditions.

**Basis:** S1's uncertainty boundary and S2's modeling restraint. Organizational
decoupling is a bibliographic research candidate, not a confirmed diagnosis.

**Test:** Obtain independent calibration/operational observations, compare
multiple plausible latent models, and report sensitivity to error/selection.
Disconfirmation includes equally well-fitting alternatives, invalid calibration
or no observable improvement. Missing data must lower what can be concluded,
not become proof of concealment.

## XFACTOR: uncertainty, proper scoring and consequence

**Question:** What omitted scenario could change a decision, and how will its
forecast and consequence be tested?

For declared binary events, use the loss convention
`Brier = mean((p_i - y_i)^2)` with frozen `p_i in [0,1]` and resolved
`y_i in {0,1}`. Lower is better. Some multicategory/sign conventions differ;
declare the exact convention before comparison. Separate calibration from
sharpness and analytical confidence. Use the existing
[forecasting procedure](../VesselFramework_Forecasting_SKILL_v1.0.md), not a
new parallel forecast ledger.

**Conditional mathematical demonstration:** If Y is Bernoulli(q), a reported
probability p has expected squared loss
`E[(p-Y)^2] = (p-q)^2 + q*(1-q)`. Expansion gives
`p^2 - 2*p*q + q`; the minimum over p in [0,1] is uniquely at p=q.
This checkable conditional result explains honest probability elicitation.
It does not establish q from intuition, validate forecasts on unobserved
events, or prove VessellFramework forecasting superiority.

**Basis:** S3 proper-score framing. For decision analysis, use separately
declared losses such as `expected_loss(a) = sum(p_s * loss(a,s))`; those
probabilities/losses are assumptions or estimates, not facts created by
the formula. Worst-case bounds require a justified scenario set.

**Test:** Freeze questions/horizons/resolution rules/probabilities; score all
resolved events against a base-rate/simple baseline. Predefine treatment of
unresolved/ambiguous outcomes and correlated cases. Report uncertainty,
coverage, calibration, missed severe outcomes and changes in decisions.
Disconfirmation includes worse loss, hindsight selection or unresolvable claims.

## KNOWING FIELD: human inquiry, not machine consciousness

**Question:** How do observer participation, affected people, consent,
dissent and missing perspectives shape the inquiry and its consequences?

**Basis:** S6, fully reviewed, supports the human-led theoretical lens.
S5's reviewed abstract supplies a compatible human/technology/environment
unit of inquiry, not validation of S6 or this integration.
No physical field equation, Shannon entropy value, collective agreement or
complete JSON record measures presencing. The fifth-pillar release policy
is owner-selected documentary/authorization policy.

**Framework hypothesis:** Structured voluntary inquiry may improve detection
of missing perspectives and consequential errors. This has not been tested
with participants. Human reports establish reported experience, not its
ontology; no software-generated account can substitute for participation.

**Test:** With appropriate consent and study governance, evaluate predeclared
source-grounded omission/error rates, participant-reported usefulness/burden,
preserved dissent, review latency and adverse effects. Instruments themselves
need construct-validity review. A process-completeness score is a process
measure, not an awareness scale. Assess power asymmetry and differential
participation; never punish refusal or manufacture consensus.

## Prospective comparative and operational study

This study is **NOT PERFORMED**. No sample size, power estimate or efficacy
result is asserted.

1. Preregister domain, hypotheses, primary outcomes, baseline, recruitment,
   inclusion/exclusion, analysis and stop conditions. Justify sample size with
   pilot variability, expected effect and consequence of error; do not choose
   an arbitrary count and call it powered.
2. Start with synthetic/public held-out cases. Compare provenance-only
   assessment, four-pillar analysis and five-pillar analysis on equivalent
   evidence. Ablation outputs are research previews, never a route around
   mandatory human completion before live report release.
3. Freeze source snapshots, method versions, prompts, model/environment,
   case allocation and scoring conventions. Randomize/counterbalance as
   appropriate; account for repeated reviewers and correlated cases.
4. Have independent domain reviewers adjudicate source support with a
   documented rubric. Blinded coding and inter-rater disagreement characterize
   scoring reliability; agreement itself does not establish truth.
5. Measure unsupported consequential claims, source/claim trace quality,
   detected omissions and forecast loss where events are resolvable.
   Separately measure time, human burden, failure/rejection, costs, correction
   rates and adverse consequences. Avoid treating one composite score as
   all of these outcomes.
6. Publish effect estimates with design-appropriate uncertainty, missingness,
   limitations, null/adverse results and generalization bounds. A new site,
   domain or population needs a new external-validity assessment.
7. Preserve independent execution, consent and release authority. A live
   paid/multiuser pilot requires production identity/isolation work that the
   current single-operator stack has not completed.

The prior 783 Python, 14 workspace and 19 dashboard passing tests establish
software behavior in their test scope. They are not participant samples,
forecast-calibration trials or evidence of improved human decision outcomes.

## Digital evidence: what can be proved now

The repository's [SHA manifest](../VessellFramework_v3.8.1_SHA256_Manifest.json)
and [verifier](../verify_manifest.py) provide byte-integrity checks relative
to that manifest. Current ambient source/lifecycle/filing/inquiry hashes bind
records and detect specified drift. They are unsigned local records, not
independent attestations against an administrator rewriting all state.
GitHub publication provides a retrievable version; it is not a blockchain
timestamp, independent replication or validated theory.

A digital dossier should preserve the exact claim, sources/access levels,
model/assumptions, test plan/results, uncertainty, artifacts/environment,
digests, corrections and verification status. Later, an independently
verified timestamp may establish a prior-existence bound; a signature may
establish key authorization with a verified identity binding; repeatable
computation may establish reproducibility. Each supports a different claim.
None upgrades unsupported empirical/causal/theoretical assertions.

**Current boundary:** No universal per-invocation signed receipt, Merkle batch,
external timestamp proof, chain attestation, wallet, payment adapter or mining
reward exists in this release. The new skill supplies a dossier template and
research method; it does not add a machine-enforced scientific output schema.
Original source bytes and historical hash contracts remain unchanged.
