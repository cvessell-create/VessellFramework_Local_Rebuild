# Personal, family and creator protection guide

Status: OWNER-ORIENTED DRAFT / RESEARCH AND PROPOSED POLICY ONLY.
Prepared October 10, 2026. Not legal advice, an executed agreement, a
registration, a safety certification or a finding against any person.

This new document develops the owner's private 18-page "Repo Protections:
Laws, Workflows & Decisions" draft. The original PDF remains unchanged and
outside the repository. This guide is a derivative, not owner-source history.
No private incident, household identity or allegation is published here.

## 1. The owner's advantage: preserve options, not invent powers

The purpose is to protect the affected person's privacy, safety, identity,
creative work and ability to make an informed response. Strong protection
comes from matching a documented problem to a real remedy, retaining useful
evidence, avoiding unnecessary disclosure and not forfeiting choices casually.

The following are proposed commitments for the project and future agent:

> My permission to use open-source code is not blanket permission to access
> private records, impersonate me or my family, or claim my endorsement.
> The permission actually granted by the applicable license still applies.
> I retain rights not granted, to the extent the law recognizes them.
>
> I will distinguish licensed use, criticism and other lawful conduct from
> conduct that may exceed a permission or violate a right. I will preserve
> evidence, request proportionate correction where appropriate and consider
> lawful remedies on their actual requirements.
>
> My private records and each family member's records are not public project
> inputs. No family relationship, account payment or administrative role
> supplies blanket consent to collection, disclosure or action.

These statements do not themselves bind a stranger, change platform terms,
create damages, overcome lawful exceptions or amend an existing license.
An implemented control, applicable statute and agreed contract have different
effects. The guide must always identify which one is doing the work.

## 2. Four distinct sources of practical leverage

| Source | What can make it meaningful | What this guide cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing legal right | Applicable elements, evidence, standing, jurisdiction, timely procedure and available remedy | Declare all elements satisfied or guarantee enforcement |
| Existing license or platform process | Actual license conditions or a verified reporting route with the required facts | Turn every objection into infringement or compel a platform result |
| Separate negotiated agreement | Identified parties, informed assent, lawful scope, clear obligations and appropriate remedies | Bind anyone merely because a policy appears in a repository |
| Protective product control | Demonstrated isolation, consent, redaction, safe approval, deletion and error handling | Treat a specification or passing checklist as implemented protection |

The agent must not calculate a legal "success score" from incomplete inputs.
If relevant facts are absent, record NOT_SUPPLIED; if a source cannot be checked,
record UNVERIFIED. Other explanations and applicable defenses are part of a
strong case assessment, not disloyalty to the owner.

## 3. Existing code permissions: retain the real conditions

The [Apache license](../LICENSE) remains unchanged.

- Section 2 grants broad copyright permissions. Corporate copying or use is
  not automatically wrongful because the owner dislikes the user.
- Section 4 conditions redistribution on a license copy, change notices,
  retained applicable notices and, where a NOTICE is supplied, its prescribed
  attribution treatment.
- Section 6 does not grant trademark permission beyond its stated origin and
  NOTICE uses. That is not a blanket ban on truthful references or criticism.
- Internal use, distribution, private data access, endorsement and model
  training are different questions. This guide does not resolve AI-training
  legality or impose a retroactive training prohibition.

Public code and confidential material also need distinct treatment. The
[trade-secret notice](trade-secrets-notice.md) separates public repository
contents from potentially qualifying confidential information; this guide
does not certify any secret or prohibit lawful independent development.
Do not disclose the supposedly confidential material in a public complaint
to prove its secrecy.

### A stronger license-compliance request

Before claiming a breach, identify the precise distributed work, upstream
revision, copyright ownership basis, third-party portions, recipient context,
missing condition and requested correction. Keep the full context and a
side-by-side exhibit, not just a screenshot of an absent credit.

Ask for the specific cure supported by the license: for example, restore an
applicable notice or include the license in the distribution. A proposed
response date is a requested deadline, not a statutory deadline. A cure request
does not itself adjudicate infringement, waive a claim or toll a filing period.
Review settlements, releases and admissions with counsel before acceptance.

Do not add "no commercial use," mandatory royalties, forced renaming or a
general ban on copying to Apache-distributed contents through this guide.
Separately controlled future services or confidential material can have
separately reviewed terms; their scope cannot silently include already-granted
permissions or rights the owner does not hold.

## 4. Remedies with actual requirements

The authorities below support only the inspected text. Current case law,
jurisdiction, procedural defenses and application to any incident remain
unverified unless separately recorded.

| Route | Concrete leverage | Conditions and risks to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copyright registration | Sections 410-412 address evidentiary significance, litigation prerequisites and eligibility for certain remedies. Timing can matter before a dispute develops. | Identify the work, publication and ownership correctly. Filing an application is not necessarily completed registration; exceptions and court interpretation need review. No owner registration search performed. |
| Copyright notice | Section 401(d) gives a qualifying notice specific evidentiary significance concerning an innocent-infringement damages defense. | Not proof of authorship or an automatic bar to every defense; statutory form/access conditions and exceptions matter. |
| DMCA notice | A copyright owner or authorized agent can use the notice process without prior registration, according to the Copyright Office. | Identify infringement, permissions and fair use accurately. Being pictured does not necessarily mean owning the photograph. A provider's safe-harbor incentives do not guarantee removal. |
| Washington consumer protection | RCW 19.86.090 permits qualifying business/property-injury claims seeking injunctions and/or actual damages, suit costs and reasonable attorney fees; discretionary increased damages are addressed in the text. | Not an award for every unfair experience or emotional harm. Trade/commerce, injury, causation, public-interest and other applicable requirements need review. Do not invent nationwide jurisdiction or promised recovery. |
| Washington electronic impersonation | RCW 4.24.790 provides an injured actual person a possible civil route for specified intentional, nonconsensual, deceptive impersonation causing injury. | Intent, platform category, causation, exemptions and provider-liability limits are express requirements. The statute does not establish anyone's motive. |
| Washington personality rights | RCW 63.60.050/.060 address specified unauthorized commercial/advertising uses, including forged digital likeness, and possible injunctions and damages. | RCW 63.60.070 has substantial exclusions. Prevailing-party fees can run against a losing claimant as well as benefit one. Do not treat every name mention as unlawful. |
| GitHub private-information removal | A narrowly identified security-risk disclosure can be submitted under the platform's dedicated process. | It is an exceptional platform service, not a general objection or copyright route. Explain the specific risk and avoid amplifying the disclosed secret. |
| GitHub trademark reporting | The inspected policy addresses misleading or confusing business affiliation and asks for specified identity/registration information. | A matching username alone is not a violation. The ordinary route requests registration; accurately disclose an unregistered name rather than inventing a number or assuming identical treatment. |

### Stronger remedies do not mean inflated amounts

RCW 19.86.090 describes discretionary increases up to three times actual damages
and a $25,000 limit on the increased award for a violation of RCW 19.86.020.
This is not a guaranteed payment, automatic minimum or universal cap on every
remedy. RCW 63.60.060 lists a $3,000 civil penalty, actual damages and specified
profits, and additional noneconomic damages for forged digital likeness;
it also addresses what counts as one act for statutory-damages computation.
Do not multiply those figures by views, screenshots or family members without
a legal basis.

Section 412 makes timing significant for statutory damages and attorney fees,
with specified exceptions and a three-month-after-first-publication condition.
The guide should flag this for prompt professional review rather than
calculating eligibility from an unverified publication date.

### Defensive protection matters too

Preserve notices received, prior permissions and the exact material disabled.
A counter-notice involves substantive assertions, personal contact information,
jurisdiction and service consequences; it is not a harmless appeal button.
Knowingly materially false copyright notices can create liability. Copyright
Office guidance describes the counter-notice process and conditional
reinstatement window; it does not guarantee an outcome for every provider.
Do not use a copyright report to bypass an impersonation or privacy standard.

## 5. A private evidence package that strengthens rather than exposes

For each issue, record only what is necessary:

1. The affected person's chosen identifier, rights/authority basis and permitted
   helper; do not collect government ID by default.
2. Original source location, relevant revision, observed time and capture method.
   Keep clock assertions distinct from a verified time service.
3. Exact conduct reported, verbatim relevant material and surrounding context.
   Separate observation, reported concern, inference and unknown attribution.
4. Permission or policy in force at the relevant time, plus later changes.
5. Actual loss, contact, disruption or confusion, supported by records where
   available. Do not invent intent, costs, participants or causal attribution.
6. Alternative explanations, defenses, exclusions and facts adverse to a claim.
7. Requested narrow remedy, potential recipient, privacy exposure and approval.
8. Submission receipt, real response, correction and unresolved outcomes.

Hashes support byte comparisons. They do not independently establish who
authored a document, whether an allegation is true or when an event happened.
A public record is not independent corroboration of another copy of that record.
An unanswered request is not an admission; no response deadline or silence rule
is manufactured by this guide.

Use separate redacted sharing copies. Avoid public GitHub issues for addresses,
children's details, health records, personal credentials, unfiled legal strategy
or identity documents. Retention/deletion and legal-hold questions need explicit
review; "supersede, never erase" must not mean keeping private data forever.

## 6. Proposed privacy and family commitments

These are design commitments and potential terms for counsel review, not a
present assurance that the software implements them:

- Collect only deliberately supplied, purpose-scoped data; no ambient capture,
  covert location tracking, keylogging or default household surveillance.
- Separate accounts and cases before any family sharing. A paying account or
  guardian role does not automatically expose another person's records.
- Explain recipient, purpose, duration and fields before consent. Declining or
  revoking permission must not alert a potentially unsafe contact.
- Do not reuse private records for model training, advertising or unrelated
  profiling as a default product practice. Any future proposed secondary use
  requires separate informed review and a lawful basis; no forced assumption of
  consent through family membership.
- Identify external processors and actual network behavior. Local storage alone
  does not establish that runtime loading, telemetry or models are local.
- Preview every export and account change; require separate explicit approval
  for the exact payload, recipient and action.
- Give a defined retention choice and deletion/export process, with documented
  exceptions and backup treatment rather than promises of perfect erasure.
- Offer safe communication choices and no default notifications carrying
  sensitive case details.
- Do not make representations about encryption, isolation, deletion, security
  certification or "no data leaves your device" before measuring them.

### Negotiated terms worth reviewing

For a future service provider, counsel can evaluate purpose limitation,
confidentiality, processor/subprocessor access, incident-notification duties,
export/deletion assistance, ownership of supplied records, prohibited secondary
use, audit evidence and remedies for actual breach. Explicitly negotiate any
indemnity, liability cap, arbitration, forum, waiver or liquidated-damages term;
do not assume a unilateral webpage binds the provider or that an extreme
one-sided clause is enforceable.

Keep these agreements separate from the public source-code license. A
privacy-first service agreement is not retroactive revocation of Apache rights.

## 7. Safe response workflow: choose the least exposing effective route

1. **Safety before technical cleanup.** Ask whether the device, channel and
   contact are safe. Do not automatically remove monitoring software, confront
   someone or alert family.
2. **Choose the affected person and authority.** Helpers need scoped consent;
   child/guardian authority requires separate legal and safety assessment.
3. **Preserve minimum necessary evidence.** Avoid new exposure or unauthorized
   access while collecting it.
4. **Sort the harm and requested outcome.** Privacy removal, account recovery,
   license cure, copyright, impersonation and consumer deception are separate
   routes. Multiple issues can share facts without becoming one filing.
5. **Verify the real route.** Open current official policy/statute and identify
   actor, eligibility, deadlines, required information and exceptions.
6. **Consider professional support early.** Threats, coercive control,
   sensitive disclosures, filings under perjury and litigation warrant review
   appropriate to the facts. No agent is an emergency responder.
7. **Review exact payload and exposure.** Do not contact the disputed party by
   default if contact could be unsafe.
8. **Explicit approval, then actual outcome.** Preparing or approving a draft
   is not a confirmed submission or successful remedy.
9. **Review results and remaining options.** Do not agree to broad waivers,
   admissions or releases without understanding their consequences.

FTC safety guidance already inspected for the
[personal/family specification](personal-family-agent-spec.md) warns that
removing stalkerware can alert an abusive person and increase danger. This is
a reason to design safe, consented help, not a claim that any user's device is
monitored.

| A child's identity misuse | FTC guidance describes checking with the bureaus, contacting companies where fraud occurred, seeking removal of fraudulent accounts and written confirmation that the child is not responsible. | Verify authority and each bureau's current documentation process privately. The practical strength is a documented mismatch between a representation and
observed behavior, a specific request through the correct process, and retained
receipts and written responses. Historical FTC settlement remedies do not become
private contractual rights by copying them into this document. No current
penalty amount or automatic compensation is asserted.

The parallel pass also opened a 2019 Retina-X release and a 2023 COPPA Rule
snapshot. These are historical research leads, not independent corroboration of
any private incident or a complete current child-privacy compliance assessment.
The 2023 rule excerpt is insufficient to design a 2026 child-facing service:
current amendments, applicability, consent exceptions and enforcement provisions
need separate verification before implementation. COPPA is not adopted here as
a general family-surveillance permission or a guaranteed private lawsuit.

### Human-reviewed request templates

These are factual starting points, not sworn legal notices. Do not send them
automatically or substitute them for a platform's required statutory form.
Only include facts and authority actually established.

#### License correction

> I am requesting review of [specific distribution and revision]. My basis for
> authority is [documented role]. The relevant upstream material is [location].
> The attached comparison identifies [specific license condition and apparent
> omission]. Please review and, if applicable, correct [precise omission].
> Please confirm the change or explain the basis for your current treatment.
> I have not concluded that other uses of the material are unauthorized.

#### Privacy/security-risk removal

> The specific material is at [private-to-recipient URL/file location].
> It contains [minimal description, not the secret itself]. The particular
> security risk is [supported explanation]. My authority to request review is
> [affected person or scoped representative]. I request [narrow removal or
> redaction] through your private-information process. Please use [safe channel]
> for questions; do not include the sensitive content in public responses.

#### Data-use or deceptive-representation inquiry

> Your statement on [date/source] says [exact relevant representation].
> The records I can provide show [observation, not assumed motive].
> Please explain the apparent discrepancy, the applicable data-use basis and
> the available correction or complaint route. I request [specific outcome].
> This is not consent to a new use or an admission that the reported facts
> satisfy every legal element.

#### Confusion or impersonation report

> The material at [location] appears to represent [specific identity or
> affiliation]. The exact representation is [relevant words/image description].
> My authority is [verified affected person/rights holder/representative].
> Evidence of actual or likely confusion is [source-linked facts].
> Please assess under [verified relevant policy] and consider [narrow action].
> I have distinguished truthful references, commentary and parody.

Omit unsafe contact details. Requesting confidentiality does not guarantee
confidential handling; verify recipient practices before disclosure.
Do not add a perjury declaration, registration number, statutory deadline,
settlement demand or legal conclusion that has not been reviewed.

## 8. Proposed implementation requirements, not new code

The [personal/family specification](personal-family-agent-spec.md) controls the
research-only product proposal. Existing single-owner services must not be
exposed as a family-isolated product.

Future acceptance tests should demonstrate:

- Unshared records cannot be found through API, UI, logs, caches or exports.
- Consent is person/purpose/recipient scoped, expires and can be revoked.
- An analysis cannot authorize execution, supply corroboration or release itself.
- Deception assessments show the actual representation and evidence, not
  an inferred malicious actor.
- A legal reference cannot elevate a missing factual element to established.
- A request sent, receipt received and remedy completed are separate states.
- Sensitive notifications, monitored-device scenarios and deletion of derived
  copies are tested explicitly.
- The source owner, derivative editor, affected person and approver remain
  separately attributed.

No new runtime capability, legal filing, account access, surveillance or
external communication is authorized by this guide.

## 10. Decisions reserved to the owner

- Which people and assistance areas the first product will serve.
- Private storage, identity, key custody, network exposure and family sharing.
- Whether to seek registration, counsel review or a separate service agreement.
- Whether and where to share a redacted incident or file a particular report.
- Whether to adopt a public notice or policy after accuracy and license review.

The current decision is to research and draft, not implement or publish.
An owner's viewpoint directs the goals; evidence and applicable law determine
which remedies can legitimately support them.
