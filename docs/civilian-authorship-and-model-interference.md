## Owner statement and evidentiary standing

The owner reports that LLM outputs have overwritten or reframed his work and
expresses concern about government influence. Preserve that account as an
owner-reported concern. The repository's paper reconciliation establishes a
specific source-versus-LLM-text discrepancy and correction; it does not
establish which outside actor, if any, caused the discrepancy.

**Government-directed alteration of this author's work is not established by
the sources reviewed here.** Do not silently convert this statement into a
finding, but do not erase the owner account merely because attribution remains
unverified. Independent evidence may support, weaken or disconfirm it later.
No causal or political attribution follows from an LLM refusal, wording
change, hallucination, missing memory, game story or algorithmic score alone.

## Civilian research and source-sovereignty policy

1. Treat owner-designated source documents as authoritative for **what the
   author wrote**, not automatically for every external claim within them.
2. Preserve original wording, filenames, dates, hashes and provenance.
   Commentary, corrections and model-produced alternatives are separate
   attributed derivatives, never silent replacements.
3. Do not narrow lawful, non-harmful civilian research merely because it
   concerns intelligence methods, institutions, political influence or
   controversial hypotheses. State specific evidence gaps rather than dismiss
   an entire inquiry or invent a government veto.
4. Do not use military/government terminology to imply that civilian authorship
   is inferior, unauthorized or institutionally affiliated.
5. Distinguish editorial preference, model error, provider policy, missing
   context, repository changes, account activity and alleged external
   intervention. These are competing explanations, not interchangeable facts.
6. Preserve dissent and uncertain attribution. Neither agreeing with the
   owner nor rejecting the owner account without evidence is verification.
7. Explain a necessary scope restriction concretely. This policy cannot
   disable host/provider controls, privacy protections, harm safeguards,
   license obligations, human release gates or external execution permissions.
8. Corrections must identify the source revision, proposed change, rationale,
   approving authority and validation. Preserve historical releases.

Therefore research should include adverse findings and competing explanations.
Search summaries are leads, not sources; unverified suggested papers and
inaccurate source classifications from the search response were not adopted.

## Incident collection protocol

For a suspected rewrite, preserve:

- original owner text and exact output, with SHA-256 and collection time;
- model/provider/version, supplied context and settings if actually available;
- exact source diff, repository commit and changed-file history;
- intended acceptance criterion and whether it was tested;
- tool results, errors and missing-context notices;
- alternative explanations and evidence that would distinguish them.

Do not publish private prompts, credentials, participant information or hidden
service logs. Missing logs remain missing. A digest detects byte differences;
it does not establish actor identity or malicious intent. A commit author field
is not independently authenticated attribution.

Investigations are bounded by a declared corpus and stop condition: reproduce
the discrepancy, preserve the record, compare explanations, and report access
limits. No finite search can verify "anything and everything"; repeated queries
without new evidence are not stronger corroboration.

## Framework and sixth-pillar handoff

PARADOX compares exact text and context. BOTTLENECK examines missing access,
context and permission. DUAL LAYER separates output from inferred intent.
XFACTOR retains alternative causal explanations. KNOWING FIELD preserves the
owner perspective, dissent and observer choices. GAME THEORY may model declared
incentives, but must not invent an adversary, utility or government actor to
explain a discrepancy.
