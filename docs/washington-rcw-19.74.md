# State Electronic Transactions Laws

Washington's Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (**chapter 1.80 RCW**),
Missouri's Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (Mo. Rev. Stat.
§§ 432.200–432.295), and California's electronic transactions title
(Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1633.1–1633.17) address electronic records, signatures
and contracts within their respective scopes.

**Citation correction, October 10, 2026:** the previous RCW 19.74 reference
returned "Citation ... cannot be found" on the official Legislature site.
The opened text of RCW 1.80.060 supplies the electronic-form recognition rule,
RCW 1.80.040 addresses agreement to transact electronically, and RCW 1.80.900
names the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act. This document keeps its
historical filename to avoid breaking links; the filename is not a citation.

California section 1633.7 addresses recognition; section 1633.5 requires
agreement to transact electronically and includes specific consent restrictions.
Section 1633.3 lists exclusions. Washington RCW 1.80.040 and Missouri section
432.220 likewise require agreement within their scopes.
